Temple Identity & Sacred Sanctity

Sri Maha Kali Ma Bahulamuki Temple is a consecrated sanctuary situated at 172, Girivalam Road, Adi Annamalai, Thiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu 606604, India, an ancient epicenter of Shiva-Shakti consciousness. The temple is reverently dedicated to the fierce, protective grace of Divine Mother Maha Kali and Devi Bagalamukhi (Bahulamuki) — the eighth of the Ten Mahavidyas who paralyzes malevolent energies, crushes negative forces, and bestows spiritual victory.

All ceremonies, invocations, and offerings conducted under the temple’s auspices strictly follow traditional Agamic, Tantrik, and Vedic sampradayas (lineages) passed down through initiated priests and aghora tantrik masters.

Tantrik rituals involve personalized spiritual sankalpam and perishable ritual commodities (flowers, fruits, homa dravyas, lemons, oils, and sacrificial offerings) procured exclusively for your designated muhurtham. Once preparation or ritual invocation begins, no refunds can be issued under any circumstances.

  • Failed / Duplicate Transactions: If your account has been debited twice due to technical network errors without booking generation, the duplicate amount will be refunded automatically to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.
